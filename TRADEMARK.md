## 2. Bundling & Commercial Distribution Requirements
Under **GPLv3 Section 7(c)** (prohibiting misrepresentation of the origin of the material) and **Section 7(e)** (declining to grant rights under trademark law):

If you distribute, package, resell, or bundle Opening Fenix alongside third-party opening courses, repertoires, books, videos, or other commercial products or services, you **must prominently display the following notice to all users and prospective buyers prior to purchase or download** (e.g. on the checkout page, sales page, product description, or marketing materials):

> **"Opening Fenix is free, open-source software available for free at github.com/Takais-Chess/Opening-Fenix"**

---

## 3. Prohibition of Misrepresentation
- **No False Exclusivity:** You may **not** state, suggest, or imply that Opening Fenix is proprietary, paid software, or exclusively obtainable through your course or product.
- **No Restrictions on Freedom:** You may **not** restrict, lock, or contractually forbid recipients from exercising their full rights under the GPLv3, including their right to redistribute Opening Fenix free of charge.
- **Enforcement:** Distributing or selling Opening Fenix without providing the required disclosure above constitutes a direct violation of GPLv3 Section 7(c) and an infringement of the project's naming and trademark rights.
